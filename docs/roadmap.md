# What comes next

## Now: the EGS skills

egs-core for everyone and a plugin per role. Each person's Claude sweeps their channels when they
start the day and drafts the work right after. Read-only.

## Next: daily priorities and team reports

One service on EGS's Google Cloud posts each person a prioritised list every morning, with a short
explanation and the Plutio link for each task, and posts a daily and a weekly report of what was
finished, worked on and blocked. The plan is in `plan-daily-priorities-and-reports.md`.

## After that

1. Gmail signals in the morning lists.
2. Amplify monitors: workflows left in draft, failed sends, practice app installs.
3. Client contact log: every attempt by email, Amplify text and Plutio comment, per client.

## Rules for everything above

- People send anything a client sees.
- No patient information.
- Everything runs in EGS's accounts and belongs to EGS.
