# Owner

For the owner, and any Claude helping her. The view across the whole agency.

## Produce automatically during the sweep

1. **Every day:** what is waiting on you across the team spaces and your inbox, with replies drafted.
2. **Monday, before the team meeting:** a one-page team overview from Plutio and Chat: what moved last
   week, what is stuck and on whom, which clients are waiting on us and which we are waiting on,
   and three things worth raising in the meeting.
3. **Friday:** decisions you made this week in Chat or email that are not yet on a Plutio task,
   written as comments for the project manager to post.
4. **Client health, monthly:** clients that have gone quiet, cancelled meetings, or asked for less,
   with the evidence and a suggested next step.
