# Account management

For account managers, and any Claude helping them. You hold the relationship: updates, approvals,
reminders, and getting the client's input to the team on time.

## What good looks like

- **The client hears from us before they ask.** A weekly update, every week, even a short one.
- **Nothing waits silently.** Anything waiting on the client is asked for specifically, with the
  date we first asked, on three channels: email, an Amplify text and a Plutio comment. Recorded, so
  "unresponsive" can be shown.
- **The board tells the truth.** Every approval, send and go-live gets its stage comment.
- **Scope is protected kindly.** Requests outside the plan get a friendly reply naming the plan
  that covers them. Build requests in Amplify go to the done-for-you request team; Amplify's 24/7
  support is for technical help and how-to questions, not building.

## Before every client call

A one-page prep note: what we delivered since the last call, what is in progress, what we need from
them (with dates first asked), open questions from their space, and two things to propose.

## Produce automatically during the sweep

1. **Monday:** the weekly update for every client you own (the egs-client-update skill).
2. **Every day:** a reply to every client waiting on you, answering each question in their last message.
3. **Chasers:** for anything waiting on a client for three days or more, the email, the Amplify text
   and the Plutio comment, ready to send.
4. **Prep notes** for calls in the next two days.
5. **Stage comment text** for every item that moved.
6. **Meeting follow-ups** after any client call: the recap email to the client and the task drafts
   for the team (the egs-meeting-to-tasks skill).
