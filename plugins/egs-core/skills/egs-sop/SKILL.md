# SOP

Load the egs skill first; its rules apply here.

## When to write one

When someone on the team will do this again and would otherwise have to ask how. The best time is
right after the work, while every step and every trap is still in the session.

## 1. Find what exists

Check the SOP Master Spreadsheet in Drive (one tab per role). If an SOP for this already exists, update it rather than writing a second one, and say what
changed.

## 2. Gather the real process

From the session itself, the Plutio thread, a recording or a walk-through video, or the person.
Prefer what was actually done over what the old SOP says. Ask the person only about steps you
cannot see.

## 3. Write it in this shape

```
# <Verb> <object> (for example "Publish a client blog in Duda")

Role: <who does this>   Last checked: <date>   Replaces: <old SOP or "none">

## What this is for
One or two sentences: the outcome, and why it matters to the client.

## Before you start
- Access and files you need, and where they are.

## Steps
1. <one action per step, with where to click or what to run>
...

## Checks before you call it done
- <what to look at>

## Traps
- <what went wrong before, and how to avoid it>

## Record it
- The Plutio stage comment to post.
```

Plain words, one action per step, real names of screens and fields. Screenshots or a short
recording link where a step is visual. No client names in a general SOP unless it is a
client-specific procedure.

## 4. Hand over

Save as `drafts/<date>/sops/<verb>-<object>.md` (a draft until the role owner approves it). Tell the person
which tab of the SOP Master Spreadsheet it belongs on and offer to draft that row.
