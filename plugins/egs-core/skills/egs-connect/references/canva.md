# Canva

**For:** social media and content. **What it gives:** Claude can find and read designs, brand kits
and folders in the person's Canva, so captions, briefs and plans match what exists. **Time:** 2
minutes. **No API key.**

## Walk-through

1. claude.ai > Customize > Connectors > Canva > Connect.
2. Sign in with the Canva account that holds EGS client designs and brand kits, and allow.

## Check

Ask Claude to find one client's brand kit or a recent design by name.

## Read-only

Canva's connector can also create and edit designs. In this setup, designs are made by a person (or
by our design partner from Claude's brief); Claude reads.
