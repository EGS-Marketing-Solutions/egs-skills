# Fathom (call recordings)

**For:** anyone whose client calls are recorded with Fathom. Team meetings are recapped in their
Google Chat space instead, which the Google sign-in already covers. **Time:** 2 minutes. **No API key.**

## Walk-through

1. claude.ai > Customize > Connectors > Fathom > Connect.
2. Sign in to Fathom with the account their calls are recorded under, and allow.

## Check

Ask Claude to list their meetings from the last week. Confirm the identity Fathom reports is their
work account and that the calls listed are EGS calls. A personal Fathom account holding other work
is not used for EGS notes.

## Read-only

Fathom's connector only reads.
