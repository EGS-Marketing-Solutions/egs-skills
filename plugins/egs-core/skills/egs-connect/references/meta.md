# Meta: Facebook and Instagram

**For:** social media, account managers with clients running ads, and the owner. **What it gives:**
read access to the pages, Instagram accounts and ad accounts their Facebook login can see: results,
posts and ad performance for reports. **Time:** 5 minutes. **No API key.**

## Before you start

Their Facebook login must already have a role on the client's page and ad account in Meta Business
Suite (Settings > People). If it does not, the client or the account owner adds them first.

## Walk-through

1. claude.ai > Customize > Connectors > Meta Ads > Connect.
2. Sign in with the Facebook login that has access to client pages, and allow the pages and ad
   accounts it lists.

## Check

Ask Claude to list the ad accounts and pages it can see. Confirm the clients they work on appear.

## Read-only

The connector can create, change and launch ads. This setup only reads; the installer blocks the
write tools in Claude Code. Campaign changes are made by the ads team or a person in Meta.
