# Social media and short video

For the social media manager and content creators, and any Claude helping them. It covers how
social works at EGS, what good looks like on each platform, and what you produce on your own
during the sweep.

## How social runs at EGS

1. **Monthly content plan per client,** drafted around the 20th for the next month.
2. **Client approval** of the plan, chased on three channels until it comes back.
3. **Creative brief to NLC,** our design partner, for the approved posts that need designed graphics.
4. **Scheduling** in Amplify's Social Planner, by a person. Before scheduling, check which page is
   connected: a page connected through someone's personal profile can post to the wrong page.
5. **Monthly results** per client, and a cadence adjustment if the numbers say so.
6. **Google Business Profile posts** for clients on an SEO plan without a social plan (see
   the egs-seo skill).

Everything here is drafted by Claude and done by a person: approvals sent, briefs sent, posts
scheduled.

## Content pillars for a practice

Mix these across the month, roughly in these proportions:

| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Education | 35% | What membership primary care is; seasonal health tips; what to do before a lab test |
| Membership value | 20% | What is included; price transparency; same-day access; time with the doctor |
| People and place | 20% | Meet the doctor and team; inside the clinic; a day in the practice |
| Community | 15% | Local events, partners, sponsorships, holidays |
| Proof | 10% | Reviews and stories, only with written consent |

For nonprofits and other clients, swap the pillars for theirs (mission, events, volunteers,
donors, impact), and keep the mix.

**Seasonal hooks to plan around:** open enrollment and employer benefits (November to January), New
Year health goals (January), Heart Month (February), back-to-school and sports physicals (July to
August), flu season (October onward), Breast Cancer Awareness (October), men's health (November),
the client's own events and anniversaries.

## Platform guide

| Platform | Formats and sizes | Captions |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Feed 1080x1350 (4:5); Reels and Stories 1080x1920 (9:16); carousels up to 20 slides | First line is the hook (about 125 characters show before "more"); 3 to 5 relevant hashtags; "link in bio" |
| Facebook | Image 1080x1350 or 1080x1080; Reels 9:16; link posts | Short; the link can go in the post |
| LinkedIn | Image 1200x627 or 1080x1350; document carousels | Professional angle: employers, benefits, the business side of the practice |
| Google Business Profile | Image 1200x900 (4:3); posts up to 1,500 characters | One clear call to action button: Book, Call, Learn more |
| TikTok and YouTube Shorts | 1080x1920 (9:16) | Hook in the first two seconds; captions on screen |

Every image gets alt text. Every video gets on-screen captions; most people watch muted.

## Captions

A caption has a hook, the point, and one call to action:

```
<hook: a question or a surprising fact, one line>
<two or three short lines of value>
<call to action: book a call, link in bio, comment, share>
<3 to 5 hashtags, Instagram only>
```

Write in the client's voice. No medical claims, no promised outcomes, no fear tactics.

## Short video: Reels, Shorts, TikTok

Script every video before anyone films it:

```
Title:
Length: 15 to 45 seconds
Hook (0 to 2 s): <what is said and what is on screen>
Beats:
  1. (2 to 10 s) <line> | <shot> | <on-screen text>
  2. ...
Call to action (last 3 s):
Shot list: <each shot, where, who, phone horizontal or vertical (always vertical)>
Caption and hashtags:
Music or sound: <trending or none; never copyrighted music on business accounts without a licence>
```

Good repeatable formats for practices: "Three things your doctor has time for now", "What a
membership includes, in 30 seconds", "Meet Dr <name>", "Myth or fact", "Day in the clinic".
Recorded clips from the client go in a Drive folder first; the edit list names each clip by file.

## Creative brief for NLC

One brief per client per month, listing every post that needs a designed graphic:

```
Client: <name>   Month: <month>   Brand: <brand kit link or colours, fonts, logo file>
| # | Post date | Platform and size | Headline on the graphic | Supporting text | Image direction | Reference |
Due back: <date>   Questions: <anything to confirm>
```

Keep text on graphics short (a headline and at most one line). The caption carries the rest.

## Privacy

- No patient in any photo or video without written consent recorded on the task.
- No identifying details about a patient, ever, even with a first name only.
- Comments that mention someone's health: the practice replies publicly with a general thank-you
  and an invitation to call, never with anything about their care.
- Reposting a public review still needs the reviewer's permission.

## Results, monthly

Per client and platform: reach, engagement rate (engagements divided by reach), follower change,
link clicks, the top three posts and why they worked, and one change for next month. Compare with
the month before. Read them from the platform or Amplify's Social Planner; if no data is
connected, the report lists the numbers the person needs to paste in.

## Produce automatically during the sweep

Each goes to `drafts/<date>/<client>/`.

1. **Next month's content plan,** around the 20th, for every client on a social plan: a table of
   dates, platform, pillar, format, full caption, hashtags, image or video direction. Built from
   the client's Chat space, events, last month's posts and the seasonal hooks above.
2. **The NLC creative brief** for each approved plan.
3. **Two short-video scripts** per client per month, with shot lists.
4. **Event kits** whenever a client space or task mentions an upcoming event: announcement,
   reminder a week out, day-of post, thank-you post, plus the Google Business Profile event post.
5. **Weekly Google Business Profile posts** for clients on an SEO plan without social.
6. **Approval chasers** for plans the client has not approved after three days: email, Amplify
   text and Plutio comment text, ready to send.
7. **The monthly results summary** on the first working day.

## Tools

- **Canva** (connector): find and read the client's designs and brand kits. Creating or editing
  designs is a person's step in this setup.
- **Meta** (connector): read page and ad results where the account allows.
- **Amplify Social Planner:** scheduling is done by a person in Amplify.
- **Drive:** each client's brand folder, photos and recorded clips.
- **Google Chat:** client spaces are where events, approvals and new photos show up first.
